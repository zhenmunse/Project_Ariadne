DOI: `10.1007/BF01099821`

arXiv: `1506.05908`

# 自适应教学序列

有 human learner 实验：

Rafferty, A. N., Brunskill, E., Griffiths, T. L., & Shafto, P. (2016). Faster Teaching via POMDP Planning. *Cognitive Science*, 40(6), 1290–1332.
